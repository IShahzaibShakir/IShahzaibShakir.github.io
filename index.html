I started in December 2023 with an EC-Council Linux course and, honestly, plain curiosity — how do systems actually get broken into? Two years on, that curiosity has become nine certifications and one clear direction: cloud security, on AWS.

I'm studying Cyber Security at The Islamia University of Bahawalpur (IUB-ICSSC). Outside class I run my own labs — an IAM policy that turns out too permissive, a bucket I check three times before calling it private, logs I read line by line. I post the write-ups on LinkedIn, mistakes included, because the mistakes are usually the part worth reading. More than 3,000 people follow along now.

What I'm looking for is straightforward: cloud security work where I can be useful from day one — reviewing IAM, tightening an S3 bucket, setting up logging that somebody actually reads.
